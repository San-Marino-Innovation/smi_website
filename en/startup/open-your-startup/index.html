Open your startup in San Marino

The High-Tech Enterprises regime supports innovative projects from launch to consolidation, with a preferential pathway of up to twelve years.

Why San Marino

A regime designed for innovators.

With Delegated Decree no. 101/2019 the Republic of San Marino introduced a regime dedicated to enterprises developing an innovative idea for a product, service, process, technology, organisation or business model. The aim is to promote a new entrepreneurial culture, support employment in key sectors and attract talent and capital.

  • 12 years

    Up to 12 years of preferential regime

    Three levels: 3 years as SUTI, 4 as SUTII, 5 as SAT.

  • 0% IGR for SUTI

    Reduced General Income Tax (IGR)

    Exemption for SUTI, a 4% rate for SUTII and 8% for SAT.

  • €1 per share (SUTI)

    Low share capital

    For limited liability companies: from 1 euro per share (SUTI), 10,000 euros (SUTII) and 20,000 euros (SAT).

  • €70 registration tax

    Reduced company costs

    For SUTI and SUTII, a fixed registration tax of 70 euros and exemption from the licence fee.

  • 36 months fixed-term

    Flexible work

    Fixed-term contracts up to 36 months, up to 4 project-based collaborations and remote work.

  • Dedicated residence permits

    Special permits for foreign employees and their families, residence for directors and employee shareholders.

  • Work for equity

    Company shares for employees, collaborators and consultants in exchange for work and services.

  • Incentives for investors

    Deductions and tax credits for those who contribute capital, with capital gains fully or partly exempt.

Tax incentives apply to enterprises that actually carry out their activity, with their own people, equipment and premises.

The High-Tech regime

Attestation, the gateway to the regime.

To access the benefits, the enterprise must obtain from San Marino Innovation S.p.A. the attestation of the highly innovative nature of the enterprise. The application is submitted by the promoting shareholders or, if the company already exists, by its legal representative, who must show that they have an innovative idea.

The attestation covers only the activity that is the enterprise's main and prevailing business: the innovative idea must match the activity actually carried out. San Marino Innovation checks this requirement both when the attestation is issued and at every renewal.

The attestation is valid for twelve months and can be renewed. Within 90 days of issue, the enterprise must apply for entry in the Register of High-Tech Enterprises, kept by the Economic Activities Office.

The three levels

From launch to consolidation.

Each stage of maturity corresponds to a level, with progressive requirements and taxation. At the end of each period the enterprise moves to the next level automatically.

Requirements

Who can apply.

  1. Individuals

    Promoting shareholders

    One or more promoting shareholders who intend to set up a San Marino limited company and can show they have an innovative idea.

  2. Limited companies

    Less than 24 months old

    Newly incorporated San Marino limited companies, or companies incorporated less than twenty-four months ago. The application is submitted by the legal representative.

  3. Spin-offs

    Owned by larger companies

    Companies at least 50% owned by larger enterprises, from San Marino or abroad: they can enter directly as SUTII or SAT, depending on the employees and turnover of the parent enterprise.

Rules to know

What Delegated Decree no. 159/2026 changes.

Highlight

Ban on outsourcing

Outsourcing the main activity or the technological core of the project is prohibited, including to group companies. Only ancillary, highly specialised or temporary activities are allowed. Technical management, intellectual property and coordination remain with the enterprise.

DD 101/2019 · art. 3-bis

Employment requirements

SUTII must have at least two employees and SAT at least three, for the whole duration of the status. If the requirement is not met for more than 90 days (SUTII) or 120 days (SAT), the status is suspended.

DD 101/2019 · arts. 11 and 13

Limit on profit distribution

30% for SUTI, 50% for SUTII, 70% for SAT, with ten points more where there are work-for-equity contracts. Breaches lead to suspension or revocation.

DD 101/2019 · art. 12-bis

Remote work

Remote work is allowed as an ordinary arrangement. The individual agreement is notified through the Labor Portal and does not affect the requirements on the actual performance of the activity.

DD 159/2026 · art. 14

Simplified liquidation

A faster closing procedure than the ordinary one, for IACT enterprises with no debts, assets below 15,000 euros, no pending proceedings and registered for no more than five years. If there are no objections, the company is struck off 30 days after the closing application is published.

DD 159/2026 · art. 15

Four steps, all online.

Frequently asked questions

In brief.

How long does the attestation last?

The attestation of the highly innovative nature of the enterprise is valid for twelve months and can be renewed according to the terms and procedures of the San Marino Innovation Regulation.

Can an existing company apply for the attestation?

Yes, if it is a San Marino limited company incorporated less than twenty-four months ago. In this case the application is submitted by the legal representative.

Can some activities be outsourced?

Only ancillary or support activities, highly specialised activities not available within the enterprise, and temporary activities linked to the development of the project. The main activity and the technological core of the project must remain with the enterprise.

What happens if a requirement is no longer met?

San Marino Innovation notifies the enterprise and grants thirty days to restore it. If the irregularity can be remedied, the status may be suspended for up to six months.

How much profit can an attested enterprise distribute?

Up to 30% of net profit for SUTI, 50% for SUTII and 70% for SAT. The limits rise by ten points for enterprises that have signed work-for-equity contracts.
