Authorise your DLT project

To issue tokens or provide services in tokens in the Republic of San Marino, entry in the Register of DLT Operators, kept by San Marino Innovation, is required.

On this page

  2. 01The regulatory framework
  3. 02Who must register
  4. 03How to apply
  5. 04Regulations and fees

The regulatory framework

Delegated Decree no. 138/2024.

Delegated Decree no. 138 of 29 August 2024 governs technologies based on distributed ledgers, known as Distributed Ledger Technology. Responsibilities are divided according to the type of token.

Type B tokens

Tokens other than crypto-assets

San Marino Innovation is the competent authority for these tokens and for operators that provide services exclusively in tokens of this type.

Type A tokens

Crypto-assets

The Central Bank of the Republic of San Marino is the supervisory authority for crypto-assets and for operators that also provide services in crypto-assets.

The Institute's role

  • Regulates and supervises the operators within its remit.

  • Promotes training and awareness of DLT technologies.

  • Works with the Central Bank of the Republic of San Marino.

02.10.2024

Entry into force of the Regulation of the Register of DLT Operators no. 001/2024.

Who must register

The Register of DLT Operators.

Issuing, offering or seeking admission to trading of tokens, or providing services in tokens, is only possible after entry in the Register. The four sections arise from the combination of who operates and the type of token.

The four sections of the Register of DLT Operators
Tokens other than crypto-assetsCrypto-assets
IssuersSection IIssuers of tokens other than crypto-assetsSection IIIssuers of crypto-assets
Service providersSection IIIProviders of services in tokens other than crypto-assetsSection IVProviders of crypto-asset services

A non-foreign operator must be set up as a limited company, except where it only provides independent advice on tokens.

View the Register

How to apply

Clear timescales, reasoned assessment.

  2. 01

    Start submission

    The application

    The applicant submits the application to San Marino Innovation, in accordance with the Institute's Regulation.

  3. 02

    10 days

    Completeness check

    Working days. To supply any missing information: at least 10 working days.

  4. 03

    30 days

    Decision

    From the complete application, the Institute decides on registration.

  5. 04

    5 working days

    Notification of the outcome

    Any refusal is always reasoned.

  • Authorisation to operate

    Registration is subject to authorisation to operate from the Economic Activities Office.

  • Processing fee

    Registration is subject to a processing fee set by a Directive of the Institute.

  • White paper

    A white paper is required for Sections I and II.
